Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. The short version: Lasso the app works on your machine. It sends nothing unless you create a share link or hand a prompt to an AI writer you chose. This website collects only what you type into the waitlist.

Who we are

Lasso is operated from Ontario, Canada. For anything about this policy or your information, write to support@mylasso.ca.

The Lasso app

  • Your files stay on your machine. Lasso reads the folders you choose and keeps its index, version history and settings in a local library on your computer. Indexing, ranking and suggestions run locally, and no language model is involved in them.
  • No account, no telemetry. The app does not require an account and does not send us your files, your typing, usage analytics or crash reports.
  • Accessibility access. On a Mac, Lasso asks for Accessibility permission so it can read the text before your caret and insert what you accept. That text is used on your machine to find suggestions and is not sent to us.
  • Share links. A share link is created only when you ask for one, for the scope you pick: the cited part, the whole document, or a redacted copy. A snapshot of only that part is stored privately on mylasso.ca and deleted when the link expires, at most 48 hours after it is made. You can stop sharing a link sooner in The Ranch.
  • AI writers. The AI writer is off until you choose one. If you choose ChatGPT and hand it a prompt, Lasso sends that prompt, and the values and passages the writer looks up in the folders suggestions come from, to ChatGPT through the ChatGPT app on your Mac and your own account. OpenAI handles that data under its own terms.
  • Connected apps. If you connect Outlook, Google Calendar, Notion or the Calendar app, Lasso signs in on the provider's own page and reads the items you allow, onto your machine. Those providers handle your data under their own policies. You can disconnect at any time.
  • Lasso MCP. When you connect an AI agent, it can ask for evidence from the folders you name with --scope. What Lasso returns goes to that agent and its provider, under their terms. Credential files and anything you excluded stay out.

This website

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist we collect your name, email address and the platform you chose. With the signup we also store the page you signed up from, the time, and the country your connection appears to come from. We use this to email you a confirmation code and, later, a download link, and to plan which builds to release first. We do not send newsletters and we do not sell or rent this information.

Your IP address is used for an hour to limit repeated signups, then discarded.

Storage in your browser

The site keeps a few items in your browser's own storage: your light or dark theme, the positions of the calculator sliders, a copy of the benchmark figures, and a one-visit key that prevents duplicate signups. These never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

Where the browser supports it, a service worker keeps a copy of the site's own pages so they load quickly and work offline.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel hosts this website and, like any host, handles request logs.
  • Upstash stores waitlist entries and share-link snapshots.
  • Our email provider delivers the confirmation code and the download email.

These providers may process information in Canada, the United States or other countries.

How long we keep it

Waitlist entries are kept until you receive your download link and for up to twelve months after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Confirmation codes expire after ten minutes. Share-link snapshots are deleted when the link expires, within 48 hours.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your waitlist entry by writing to support@mylasso.ca; we answer within 30 days. Right after signing up, the Undo button removes your entry at once. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as PIPEDA, the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to complain to your privacy regulator.

Children

Lasso is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, if you are on the waitlist, tell you by email.
