Lasso Box

A small box for your office. It indexes your team’s projects on site and serves share links that everyone in the building can open. No cloud in between.

It indexes the office

Plug it in and point it at your shared folders. It ropes in every project and document, right there in the building.

Why a box

Lasso for the whole office

Lasso runs on your own computer today, so share links only open on that machine. The box would give a team one shared, local index, without sending anything to a cloud service.

1Indexes on site

Projects stay in your office. The box keeps the snapshots, versions and evidence right next to your files.

2Links the whole team can open

Share the indexed part, the whole document or a redacted copy, and colleagues can open it from any desk.

3Nothing leaves the building

The same local-first Lasso, no cloud and no API keys, just on hardware the whole team shares.

Concept only. Design, specifications, pricing and availability haven’t been decided.
