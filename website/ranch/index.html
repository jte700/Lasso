The Ranch ⇧⌘R

Every source, link and change in one window

Open it from any app. Your folders, the links you inserted and every change to your files, with edits reviewed before you approve them.

Mac Apple siliconWindows

Recent Changes

What changed, and who changed it

Every save in one log: the time, who made it and the file. Open a row to see the lines, cells or values that moved, and the formulas they recalculated.

Review Changes

Review edits the way you review code

A tab for each file edited since the folder was approved. Comment on a line, cell or passage, apply a suggested change, or start a comment with \ and ChatGPT makes it. Then approve.

All Sources

Find any file you have added

Every source in one list. The search pill at the foot of the page narrows it as you type, and chips keep a folder or kind in view.

Share

Whole, redacted or marked up

A source’s Share button makes a private link and opens a message to the people you name. Choose the whole file, a redacted copy or one with your marks.
