Security and privacy

Your files stay
where they are

Lasso is retrieval, not generation. It reads only the folders you choose, keeps its index on your computer, and runs suggestions there. Two things can leave your machine, and only when you ask: a share link, and a prompt for an AI writer.

What leaves, and whenPrivacy policy

What leaves your computer, and when

DataWhere it goes
What you type, and the suggestionsStays on your computer. Indexing, ranking and suggestions run locally. No language model is involved and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
Your files, index and version historyStay on your computer, in a local library beside the folders you chose. Lasso reads your files and never edits them.
A prompt for the AI writerOnly when you hand one over with Tab. The prompt, and the values and passages the writer looks up, go to ChatGPT through the ChatGPT app on your Mac and your own account.
A share linkOnly when you create one. A snapshot of the part you chose is stored privately on mylasso.ca and deleted when the link expires, at most 48 hours later.
Lasso MCP for AI agentsOnly what a connected agent asks for, from the folders you name with --scope, goes to that agent and its provider. Credential files and anything you exclude stay out.
The waitlist on this websiteYour name, email address and platform, so we can send a confirmation code and a download link.

No account, no telemetry

The app does not need an account and does not send us your files, your typing, usage analytics or crash reports.

Accessibility, used locally

On a Mac, Lasso asks for Accessibility permission to read the text before your caret and insert what you accept. That text is used on your computer only.

Quiet where it should be

Lasso never suggests in search fields or address bars, and you can switch it off for any app in Settings.

AI writer off by default

The writer is set to None until you choose ChatGPT with ⌥Tab. With None, a backslash is only a backslash.

Shares are private and brief

Each link carries a long random token, is not indexed by search engines, and stops working within 48 hours. Stop any link sooner from The Ranch.

One client at a time

⌥Tab limits suggestions to one folder, so figures from one engagement do not appear while you write about another.

Questions

Can Lasso’s team see my files?

No. Your files and Lasso’s index stay on your computer. We only ever receive the snapshot in a share link you create, which is stored privately and expires.

Does the AI writer train on my files?

Lasso hands your prompt, and what the writer looks up for it, to ChatGPT through your own account, under OpenAI’s terms and your plan’s data settings. Nothing else from your files is sent.

What happens to a USB drive’s index when I unplug it?

Lasso asks whether to keep the index or remove it. A kept index stays on your Mac; a removed one is deleted.

Where is the legal detail?

In the privacy policy and the terms of service. Questions go to support@mylasso.ca.
