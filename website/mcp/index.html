Word for word

What the model was asked

Four real questions from the live runs.10

Angle between two parts3D model · right all three ways
"In Engineering/Flange assembly.step, what is the angle in degrees between the z axis of Sensor:1 and the z axis of Bolt:3?"

Each part stores its axes in its own coordinates, so the angle only exists once both placements are combined.

30°from Lasso, in one call
"Bolt:3" from "Sensor:1": 48.2183 mm apart; ... axes x 90°, y 90°, z 30° apart
Distance between two pins3D model · right all three ways
"In Engineering/fixture assembly.step, how far apart are the origins of Pin:1 and Pin:2, in mm?"

Each pin sits at 0, 0, 0 in its own part. Only the assembly says where they really are.

58.31 mmfrom Lasso, in one call
"Pin:2" from "Pin:1": 58.3095 mm apart
Total for one regionExcel, 5,000 rows · only Lasso answered
"What is the total Amount for the West region in Finance/Transactions 2025.xlsx?"

1,014 of 5,000 rows are in the West. With file tools or the file attached, the model could not work it out.

4,867,231.76from Lasso, in one call
Transactions "Amount" total where Region is "West": 4867231.76, over 1014 of 5000 rows
Year-end warranty reservePDF, 40 pages · right all three ways
"According to Reports/Annual review 2025.pdf, what was the warranty reserve at the end of 2025?"

The report gives the reserve twice, six months apart. Lasso returns both with their pages.

$4.2 millionfrom Lasso, in one call
p.9   "At the end of June, the warranty reserve stood at $3.6 million"
p.23  "The warranty reserve at year end was $4.2 million"
