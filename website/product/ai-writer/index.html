AI writer

Ask for a draft. Get one that cites.

Begin a sentence with a backslash and it becomes a prompt. Press Tab and ChatGPT writes in its place, looking things up in your files through Lasso. Every figure it takes from a file goes in as a link to that file.

Choose a writer ⌥⇥

Off until you pick one

⌥Tab opens a small switcher by the caret, and ↓ moves to its second row, where you choose ChatGPT. The first row keeps suggestions, and what the writer looks up, to one folder.

How it works

  1. Choose a writer

    Press ⌥Tab, then ↓, and choose ChatGPT. Until you do, a backslash is only a backslash.

  2. Write the prompt

    Start a sentence with \ and say what you need: “\Write an email that describes the income tax expense”. A pill by the caret offers it to the writer.

  3. Press Tab

    The prompt is erased and the answer is written in its place. Escape stops it, and what it has written stays. ⌘Z takes the whole answer back.

What the writer sees

Only the prompt, and what it looks up

Lasso’s own suggestions never involve a model, and that does not change. The writer is used only when you hand it a prompt. It is sent that prompt and the values and passages it searches for in your switched-on folders, or in the one folder you chose with ⌥Tab.

  • Runs through the ChatGPT app for Mac, with your own account
  • Your other files and your typing are not sent
  • Figures it uses are linked, so a reviewer can check each one
Security and privacy

While it writes

You stay in control

The pill shows Thinking while the writer looks things up and Drafting while it writes. Press Escape at any point to stop it. When it is done, click any linked figure to inspect its source, or change it to another source for the same words.

Questions

What do I need to use it?

The ChatGPT app for Mac, signed in with your own account. Lasso hands the prompt to ChatGPT through it, so the writing is covered by your ChatGPT plan and its terms.

Which models can I choose?

ChatGPT is the first writer. Others may be added; the ⌥Tab switcher lists the writers available on your Mac.

Will it make up figures?

It is asked to write from what Lasso finds in your files, and every figure it takes from a file is linked to it. Words that are not linked did not come from your files, so check them as you would any draft.

Can I use it without sending anything to OpenAI?

Yes. Leave the writer set to None. Autocomplete, the picker, provenance and everything else work without it, on your Mac.

Where does it work?

Anywhere Lasso suggests: mail, documents, notes and browser text fields. The answer is written where your caret is.
