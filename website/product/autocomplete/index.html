The exact figure, as you type it

Lasso reads the folders you choose and offers matching values and passages while you write, in any app. Each suggestion is previewed in its sheet, page, slide or recording, so you can check it before Tab puts it in.

Autocomplete

Write as usual, check, press Tab

When a word you type is in a source, the card appears under the caret with the value shown in its sheet. Tab puts it in, linked to the cell it came from.

How it works

Three steps, and you never leave the document

  1. Write as usual

    Lasso reads the sentence before the caret. When a word you typed is in a source, such as “revenue” in a forecast row, the card appears.

  2. Check the source

    The card shows the value in its sheet, the passage on its page, or the moment in a recording. The arrows move between sources.

  3. Press Tab

    The value goes in where you are typing and stays linked to the cell or passage it came from.

The card

Everything you need to trust a figure

The left side lists the sources that match, each with its file and cell or page. The right side previews the one you have chosen: the sheet with the cell outlined, the page with the sentence highlighted, the slide, or the recording at its moment.

  • Ghost text shows the rest of the value after the caret
  • A value that changed recently says so, with the old figure
  • Open file and Share link are one click away

Ranking

Exact matches come first

A direct match, such as an exact name, value or file name, always ranks above a looser one. A suggestion also needs a real word you typed that the source itself contains, not just its folder or file type, so phrases like “we found that” bring up nothing.

If nothing in your files supports what you are writing, Lasso shows nothing rather than guess.

A few keys, mostly one

Tab does almost everything

There is no new interface to learn. Suggestions appear as you type and Tab accepts them. A few other keys cover the rest.

Questions

Does a language model write the suggestions?

No. Suggestions are found in your files and ranked on your Mac. No model is involved and nothing you type is sent anywhere. The separate AI writer runs only on a prompt you hand it.

Which apps does it work in?

Lasso works where you type text: mail, word processors, notes, chat and browser text fields. You can switch it off for particular apps, and it never suggests in search fields or address bars.

How fast is it?

In our tests a suggestion took about 7 ms per keystroke with ten folders switched on, and 27 of 28 test prompts got the right suggestion first. See the benchmarks.

Can I limit suggestions to one client’s folder?

Yes. Press ⌥Tab and choose the folder; suggestions come only from there until you choose All again.

What happens if I press Escape?

The card closes and Lasso stays quiet for the rest of the sentence.
