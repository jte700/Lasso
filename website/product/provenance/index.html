Provenance

Every value keeps its source

What Lasso inserts stays tied to the file, the cell or passage, and the version it came from. Click it to see where it came from. When the file changes, you hear about it, and nothing is rewritten without your OK.

Provenance

Click a linked value

Linked words are drawn as pills in their folder’s colour. Click one to Inspect Source or Change Source. When its file changes, the link says so, with the old value and the new one, and waits for your OK.

From insert to review

  1. Linked when inserted

    Tab or ⌘Return records the file, the cell or passage, and the version. Lasso draws the words as a pill in the apps you write in.

  2. Inspected with a click

    Inspect Source opens the file at that cell or passage. Change Source lists the other sources the card offered for those words and writes the one you choose.

  3. Reviewed when it changes

    When the file is edited, Lasso shows the old value, the new one, and every sentence that cites it. Update the wording or keep the version you used.

The Ranch ⇧⌘R

Every source, link and change in one window

The Ranch opens from any app. Recent Changes lists edits to your files, with changed cells and passages marked. Links lists everything you have inserted. Needs Review holds the values whose source moved. Network shows how your files connect.

  • Changed cells tinted, with the versions to compare
  • Changed passages marked word by word
  • Shared links listed, and stopped from there

A version for every save

Lasso keeps a version history of the files it indexes, on your Mac. A one-cell edit to a large workbook adds very little; see the storage figures.

Recent changes, said plainly

A suggestion whose value changed recently says so in the card, such as 19,250 · was 18,400, so you do not copy a stale figure.

Nothing changes by itself

Lasso never edits your documents on its own. Every update to a sentence waits for you to accept it.

Questions

What is the difference between Tab and ⌘Return?

Tab inserts the way you set in Settings. ⌘Return always inserts with local source tracking and never as a share link.

Do the pills appear for other people?

No. The pills are drawn by Lasso on your screen. Someone you send the document to sees ordinary text, unless you share a link.

Can I see which documents cite a cell?

Yes. The review for a changed value lists every sentence that cites it, and The Ranch lists every link.
