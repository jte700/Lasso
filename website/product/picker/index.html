Cell and passage picker

Choose exactly what goes in

Sometimes one value is not enough. Open the sheet behind a suggestion and highlight the cells you want, or open the document behind a passage and choose its exact words. Lasso puts them in with the source attached.

Download for MacTry the demo

Spreadsheets and CSV fn ⇥

Highlight cells, paste a table

Press fn + Tab on a spreadsheet or CSV suggestion and its sheet opens on the cell the card offered. Drag across the cells you want; highlights need not touch. ⌘Z takes back the latest, and Tab pastes a picture of them, with the source written underneath if you choose.

What is pasted

A clean table that any app accepts

The picture is drawn light, whatever your system look, so it reads well in a document or an email. The header row is shaded, numbers are set right, and the cells keep where they sit in the sheet.

  • Pasted as PNG and TIFF, which mail, documents and chat apps take
  • Switch on Insert with source to write it underneath, such as (forecast_2026.xlsx, 'P&L'!D12, B14:C14)
  • Switch on Insert with the header row to put the column names first

Which files

Workbooks and exports

The sheet picker opens Excel workbooks (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV or TSV exports from your bank, ledger or practice software. A CSV has no sheet name, so its source line names the file and cells. Tab on the card itself still inserts a single value as text.

PDF, Word, decks and recordings ⇥

Check the words before they go in

Tab on a passage suggestion opens its document at the passage, highlighted from the words you typed. Select other words to change the highlight, then Insert, or Insert Link to put in a share link instead.

How it works

  1. Open it from the card

    fn + Tab on a spreadsheet or CSV suggestion opens the sheet. Tab on a passage from a PDF, Word file, deck or recording opens the document.

  2. Highlight what you need

    Drag across cells or select words. The arrow keys move the latest highlight, and with ⇧ they widen it.

  3. Put it in

    Tab pastes the cells as a picture, or inserts the words linked to their place in the file. Escape goes back to the card.

Questions

Can I paste the cells as editable text?

The sheet picker pastes a picture, so the table looks the same wherever it lands. To insert a single value as text, press Tab on the card instead of fn + Tab.

Can the source be written under the picture?

Yes. Switch on Insert with source in the picker. The line names the file, the sheet and each highlight in the order you made them, so a reader can see where the figures came from.

Does the picker change my file?

No. It reads from Lasso’s index of the file. Nothing in the workbook or document is edited.

What does ⌘Z do in the picker?

It takes back the latest highlight. ⇧⌘Z brings it back.
