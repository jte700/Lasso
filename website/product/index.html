Everything Lasso does

Lasso finds the figure you need in your own files, puts it in where you are typing, and keeps the link to its source. These are the parts, and the keys that use them.

Autocomplete

The figure, clause or passage, as you type

Lasso watches the sentence you are writing and offers matching values and passages from your folders. The card previews each one in its sheet, page, slide or recording, so you can check it before Tab puts it in.

  • Works in any app you type in
  • Exact matches always rank first
  • Ghost text shows the value after the caret
How autocomplete works

Cell and passage picker

Choose exactly what goes in

fn + Tab on a spreadsheet or CSV suggestion opens its sheet: drag over the cells you want and Tab pastes them as a table, with the source underneath. Tab on a passage opens its document to choose the exact words.

  • Several highlights, which need not touch
  • ⌘Z takes back the latest highlight
  • Pasted as a picture that mail and documents accept
See the picker

AI writer

Prompts answered from your own files

Begin a sentence with a backslash and it becomes a prompt. Tab hands it to ChatGPT, which looks things up through Lasso and writes in place, linking every figure it took from a file.

  • Uses your own ChatGPT account
  • Escape stops it; ⌘Z takes the answer back
  • Off until you choose a writer with ⌥Tab
See the AI writer

Provenance

Every value remembers where it came from

Inserted words are drawn as pills in the source folder’s colour. Click one to inspect its source or change it to another; when a file changes, Lasso lists every sentence that relied on it.

  • Pinned to the version you used
  • Change Source swaps in another source the card offered
  • Nothing is rewritten without your OK
How provenance works

Sharing

Send the reference, not the folder

⇧⌘Return turns a suggestion into a private link. Choose the cited part, the whole file or a redacted copy; each kind of file offers the ways that suit it.

  • Links expire after 48 hours
  • Redact names, rent or account numbers first
  • Stop sharing at any time from The Ranch

Listening

USB drives

Plug in a drive. Lasso asks first.

Connect a USB drive and the Lasso icon in the menu bar lights up. Index the whole drive or only the folders you choose, and they join your working directories. When you unplug it, Lasso asks whether to keep the index or remove it.

  • Any USB storage, USB-A or USB-C
  • The whole drive, or chosen folders
  • Indexed on your Mac; nothing is uploaded

Choose sources ⌥Tab

Suggestions from one client, or all of them

⌥Tab opens a small switcher by the caret. Tab moves through your folders and apps, Return keeps one, and suggestions come only from there until you choose All again. ↓ shows the second row, which chooses the AI writer.

  • Keep one engagement’s files apart from another’s
  • The choice holds until you choose All again

Recordings

Quote what was said on the call

Lasso transcribes the speech in your recordings on your Mac. Put a phrase in quotes and it looks for those exact words; the card plays the recording from the moment they were said.

  • .mov, .mp4 and .m4v
  • Transcribed on the Mac, with macOS 26

The Ranch ⇧⌘R

Every source and link in one window

The Ranch opens from any app. It lists recent changes to your files, every link you have inserted, what needs review, the links you have shared, and how your files connect.

Also

Smaller things that save time

Name a file in plain words

Type a file’s name as it is spelled and Tab completes it, or describe it and Lasso writes its readable name: the general ledger.

Cite code by line

After a code file, write line 42 or lines 10–20, and Tab puts those lines in, indented as they are.

Apps it stays quiet in

Choose apps where Lasso never suggests. Search fields and address bars are always left alone.
