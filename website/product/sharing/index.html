Ways to share

Each kind of file, shared the way that suits it

FileWhat you can share

▶ RecordingsThe whole recording, opening at the moment you cited

  1. Press ⇧⌘Return

    On a suggestion in the card, or in the passage picker. The share sheet opens with the ways this file can be shared.

  2. Choose what they see

    The cited part, the whole file, or a copy with names, rent, account numbers or cells covered first.

  3. Send as usual

    A private link goes into your message. It opens the part you chose, and expires after 48 hours.

Only what you choose leaves

Lasso uploads a snapshot of the part you chose, not the folder, and not the rest of the file unless you share the whole file.

Private and short-lived

Each link carries a long random token, is not indexed by search engines, and stops working 48 hours after it is made.

Stopped whenever you like

Every link you have shared is listed in The Loop, where you can stop it at once.

Questions

Does the person I send it to need Lasso?

No. The link opens in any browser, and they can download what you shared.

Can I make a link last longer than 48 hours?

No. Links last at most 48 hours, and you can choose an earlier expiry. Share again to send a fresh link.

Is the redaction applied to the file itself?

The shared copy is made with the covered parts blacked out, and only that copy is uploaded. Your file is not changed.

Does Tab ever share by itself?

No. Sharing only happens when you press ⇧⌘Return or choose Share link, and ⌘Return never shares.
