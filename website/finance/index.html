Spreadsheets first

Lasso reads a workbook the way you do

Lasso reads every sheet’s rows, columns and cells, with their headers, and shows a suggestion in its sheet with the cell outlined. fn → opens the row so any of its values can be chosen, and fn + Tab opens the sheet to highlight a range.

  • Excel (.xlsx, .xls), OpenDocument (.ods), CSV and TSV
  • Values shown as the sheet displays them, such as 18,400
  • Very large folders searched through a dedicated index
  • Only changed sheets are read again after an edit

Client files stay on your computer

Lasso indexes the folders you choose, on your Mac. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing leaves your machine unless you share a link or hand a prompt to an AI writer you chose.

Separate engagements

⌥Tab limits suggestions to one client’s folder, so another client’s figures never appear.

AI is off by default

The AI writer is set to None until you choose ChatGPT, and then sees only what it looks up for your prompt.

Shares you control

A shared link holds only the part you chose, can be redacted first, and expires after 48 hours.

Security in detail

Questions from finance teams

Does Lasso change my workbooks?

No. Lasso reads files to index them and never writes to them. It keeps its own version history beside them, on your Mac.

Does it work while the workbook is open in Excel?

Yes. Lasso reads the saved file. When you save a change, it reads the sheets that changed and tells you if a figure you cited has moved.

Will it suggest a figure from a draft by mistake?

Every suggestion shows its file, sheet and cell before you insert it, and stays linked after. ⌥Tab can limit suggestions to the folder that holds the final versions.

Can it read PDF bank statements?

It reads the text in PDFs. A scanned statement needs text recognition first; most bank and accounting software exports statements with text, or as CSV.

Does it work with our practice software?

Lasso works with the files that software exports, such as Excel and CSV, and in the text fields where you write. It does not connect to the software itself.
