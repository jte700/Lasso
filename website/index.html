More than autocomplete

Picker ⇥ or fn ⇥

Pick the cells or words

Tab on a PDF, Word or deck passage opens its page to highlight the exact words. fn + Tab on a spreadsheet opens its sheet, and Tab pastes the cells you highlight as a picture. Either way the source can go in with them.

AI writer \ then ⇥

An AI writer that cites

Start a sentence with a backslash and Tab hands it to ChatGPT. It writes in place from your files and links every figure it uses.
