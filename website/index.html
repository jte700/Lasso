    Retrieval first

    Stop sifting. Start finding.

    Ask for the number, the clause or the figure. Lasso ranks every indexed document and takes you to the exact cell, passage or object it lives in, not just a file it might be in.

    The exact locationCell D12, clause 12.3, IfcSpace Schlafzimmer. Not “somewhere in this PDF”.
    The right versionDrafts and superseded files are ranked and labelled, so you don’t cite the wrong one.
    No evidence, no answerIf nothing in your project supports it, Lasso says so instead of guessing.
    Write with evidence

    Autocomplete that shows its work

    Suggestions come from your own project, not from a model. Right inside the suggestion box, Lasso previews the source (the floor plan, the sheet, the paper, the clause) so you can confirm it before you press Tab. Click any highlight later to check it again.

    Review changes

    When a source changes, you hear about it

    Every linked value remembers the version it came from. When the file is updated, Lasso shows the old value, the new one, and every sentence that relied on it, then lets you update the wording or keep the historical version.

    • Changes are tracked through content-addressed snapshots
    • Review proposed wording before anything is rewritten
    • Indexing failures show up too, not just edits
    Six keys, mostly one

    Tab does almost everything

    There’s no new interface to learn. Suggestions appear as you type and Tab accepts them. A handful of other keys cover everything else. Click a row to see it played.

    Share what you cite

    Reference it. They can open it.

    Press ⇧⌘↵ on a suggestion and the reference becomes a link. Share the indexed part, the whole document, or a copy with the private details blacked out. No hunting for the file, no attaching, no redacting by hand.

    Indexed partOnly the clause, cell or passage you cited, with enough context to read it.
    Whole documentThe full file when they need it, with the original available to download.
    Redacted copyRent, account numbers, names or signatures blacked out before anyone sees them.

    Supported sources

    From prose to polylines

    Support varies by format and content. Files stay in history even when structured extraction isn’t available.

    .mdProse
    Markdown and plain textPassages, source-backed continuations and claims..md · .txt
    .xlsxFigures
    SpreadsheetsTables, rows, cells, values and variable definitions..xlsx · .csv · .tsv
    .pdfDocuments
    DOCX and PDFExtractable text and metadata; explicit drawing annotations..docx · .pdf
    .bibCitations
    Reference librariesAuthor, year, title, DOI and PMID metadata..bib · .ris · .nbib · EndNote
    .ipynbCode
    Code, JSON and notebooksCode locations, structured values and notebook cells..ipynb · .json · source
    .ifcBIM
    Building modelsElement quantities and typed properties, with entity identity and units..ifc
    .dxfCAD · Civil
    Drawings and site modelsPolyline areas and perimeters; parcel areas, alignments and pipes..dxf · .landxml
    .jpgPhotos
    Whiteboards and handwritingFound by what’s written on them.In development

    Scanned PDFs need OCR first. DWG, RVT and DGN need an export to IFC, DXF or LandXML. Lasso does not infer missing quantities.
