    Nº 01Front matter

    Lasso is a local-first source library and writing assistant1. It connects what you write to the files it came from, down to the exact cell, passage or IfcSpace.

    Most of writing is looking things up: which folder, which version, which tab, which page. Lasso does the sifting. Ask for the number, the clause or the figure, and it pulls the fragment from the archive and sets it into your sentence.

    Every fragment keeps its provenance: source object, location, version2. When a source changes, Lasso issues an erratum: the old value, the new one, and every sentence that relied on it3.

    No language model. No API key. Retrieval, ranking, extraction and provenance run on your machine. Ambiguous measurements aren’t guessed4. If the evidence isn’t in the archive, neither is the answer.

    Nothing invented. Everything sourced.

    Nº 02The catalog

    Stop sifting. Ask the archive.

    Ask for the number, the clause or the figure. Lasso riffles through every indexed document and pulls the card for the exact cell, passage or object, with drafts and superseded versions flagged.

    Ask
    The exact locationCell D12, clause 12.3, IfcSpace Schlafzimmer. Not “somewhere in this PDF”.
    The right versionDrafts and superseded files are ranked and labelled so you don’t cite the wrong one.
    No evidence, no answerIf nothing in your project supports it, Lasso says so instead of guessing.

    Nº 04Dispatch

    Cite it. Send it.
    They can open it.

    Press ⇧⌘↵ on a reference and it becomes a link. Share the indexed part, the whole document, or a copy with the sensitive lines inked out. No hunting for the file, no attaching, no redacting by hand. The recipient clicks the highlight and gets the source, pinned to the version you cited.

    Nº 06Specimen of sources

    From prose to polylines.

    Support varies by format and content. Files stay in history even when structured extraction isn’t available.

    .md .txtPassages, source-backed continuations and claimsProse
    .xlsx .csv .tsvTables, rows, cells, values and variable definitionsFigures
    .docx .pdfExtractable text and metadata; explicit drawing annotations in PDFsDocuments
    .ipynb .jsonCode locations, structured values and notebook cellsCode
    .bib .ris .nbibReference records with author, year, title, DOI and PMIDCitations
    .ifcElement quantities and typed properties, with entity identity and unitsBIM
    .dxfArea and perimeter of supported closed model-space polylinesCAD
    .landxmlParcel areas, alignment and pipe lengths, diameters and elevationsCivil
    .jpg .heicWhiteboards and handwriting, found by what’s written on themIn development

    Scanned PDFs need OCR first. DWG, RVT and DGN need an export to IFC, DXF or LandXML. Lasso does not infer missing quantities.
