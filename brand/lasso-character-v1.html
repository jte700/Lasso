Lasso · brand character

Meet Lasso.

A small, curious wrangler with one job: catch the exact fragment you need and bring it back with its source attached. It only catches what is really there.

Click anywhere on the stage to throw it something to catch.

Pose sheet

Six moves, one job.

Personality

Curious, quick, honest.

CuriousIts eyes are always on what you’re writing, and on whatever drifts past that might belong in it.
PreciseIt lassos the exact fragment (a cell, a clause, a room), never the whole drawer.
HonestNo source, no catch. If the evidence isn’t there, it comes back empty-handed and says so.

Construction

How it’s drawn.

  • 01One flat colour for the body. No outlines, gradients or shading.
  • 02Hand-cut edges that “boil”: the silhouette is redrawn about nine times a second.
  • 03The rope is always in the off-colour: cream on ink, ink on paper and lime.
  • 04Two oval eyes, no nose. A mouth only appears for surprise or delight.
  • 05Squash on every landing, stretch on every jump. Nothing moves stiffly.
  • 06Whatever it catches gets a source tag. Lasso never holds anything unlabelled.
