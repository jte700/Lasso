Personality

Curious, quick, honest.

CuriousTheir eyes are always on what you’re writing, and on whatever drifts past that might belong in it.
PreciseThey rope the exact fragment (a cell, a clause, a room), never the whole drawer.
HonestNo source, no catch. If the evidence isn’t there, they ride back empty-handed and say so.

Construction

How they’re drawn.

  • 01A lopsided pebble of flat colour on two little feet. No outlines, gradients or shading.
  • 02Hand-cut edges that “boil”: the silhouette is redrawn about nine times a second.
  • 03The hat is part of the character. It floats on every jump and tips when they land a catch.
  • 04Cowboy: creased hat, optional polka-dot bandana. Cowgirl: upturned hat with a star, braids, optional bow.
  • 05The rope is always in the off-colour: cream on ink, ink on paper.
  • 06Whatever they catch gets a source tag. Nothing is ever held unlabelled.
